Business Logic Guide

  • R1
    Shift Dating: Punches are attributed to the shift start date in IST. Overnight shifts belonging to yesterday morning count correctly.
  • R2
    Grace Period: Late minutes are counted only if arrival is strictly > 10 mins after shift start.
  • R3
    Overtime: Counted only if punch-out is ≥ 30 mins after shift end.
  • R4
    Work Hours: Rounded half-up to 2 decimals using exact Decimal math.
  • R5
    Half-Day: Flagged automatically when completed work hours < 4.5 hours.
